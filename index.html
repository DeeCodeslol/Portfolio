About Me

I'm a UX researcher and designer who believes the best digital experiences are built on genuine curiosity about people. Currently pursuing my BS in Technical Communication (User Experience) at Arizona State University while accelerating into my master's, I bring research rigor, visual systems thinking, and a deep commitment to making products that actually work for the humans using them.

What Drives Me

I've been a henna artist for years, which taught me something design school reinforces every day: great craft lives in the details, and the best work makes people feel something. That same obsession with intention and precision follows me into every usability evaluation, wireframe, and research session I run. I care about people in my work the same way I care about them in my life, and that shows up in designs that feel thoughtful rather than just functional.
