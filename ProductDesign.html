Changemaker Promotional Product Design

A collection of branded promotional materials I designed for Changemaker at ASU West Valley to support student engagement, event visibility, and campus outreach.

← Back to Projects View My Work
Branding Print Design Event Promotion Campus Marketing

Project Details

  • Role: Designer & Student Leader
  • Organization: Changemaker at ASU West Valley
  • Tools: Canva, Google Suite
  • Focus: Promotional products, visual consistency, student engagement

Overview

In my work with Changemaker, I design promotional product concepts and branded materials that help communicate event themes, create a recognizable visual identity, and make student-facing experiences feel more polished and memorable.

What I Designed

  • Branded giveaway concepts
  • Event promotional materials
  • Student-facing visual assets
  • Graphics aligned with Changemaker branding

What This Work Shows

  • Ability to design for real campus audiences
  • Understanding of branding across formats
  • Attention to visual cohesion and usability
  • Creative problem-solving within organizational needs

Design Approach

Each piece is designed to feel approachable, engaging, and aligned with Changemaker’s mission while still being functional for promotional use.

Considerations

  • Audience appeal for ASU students
  • Clear, recognizable branding
  • Visual consistency across products
  • Practicality for events and outreach

Goals

  • Increase event visibility
  • Create memorable takeaways
  • Strengthen Changemaker’s campus presence
  • Design assets that feel polished and intentional
