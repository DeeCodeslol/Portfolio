Project Details

  • Role UX Researcher & Designer
  • Site mabeproducts.com
  • Methods Pre/post questionnaires, usability testing
  • Focus Task success, clarity, satisfaction

Overview

The goal of this project was to evaluate the usability of mabeproducts.com with real users, identify where the experience broke down, and use that data to drive targeted redesign decisions that measurably improved the site.

The Problem

  • Users struggled to navigate to key product and information pages
  • Visual hierarchy and layout made it difficult to identify primary actions
  • No existing data confirmed or quantified the experience gaps

The Goals

  • Establish a usability baseline with pre-test data before any changes
  • Run moderated usability sessions to observe real user behavior
  • Measure improvement with post-test data after redesign

Research Process

A four-stage process ensured every redesign decision was grounded in real user data rather than assumption.

1

Pre-Test Questionnaire

Collected baseline data on participants' prior experience with the site, general expectations, and confidence levels before any tasks were introduced.

2

Usability Sessions

Moderated testing sessions where participants completed key tasks on the live site while thinking aloud, surfacing real-time friction and confusion.

3

Post-Test Questionnaire

Captured satisfaction scores, perceived ease of use, and specific pain points immediately after task completion to quantify the experience.

4

Analysis and Redesign

Synthesized quantitative questionnaire data and qualitative session observations to identify the highest-impact changes and implement them.

Findings and Redesign Decisions

Pre and post-test data pointed to clear, recurring patterns. Each finding directly informed a specific redesign change.

Finding

Navigation Confusion

Users had difficulty locating product categories and key pages. Multiple participants took incorrect paths before finding their target, indicating unclear labeling and menu structure.

Redesign

Simplified Navigation Structure

Reorganized menu labels to reflect how users described the content, reducing cognitive load and making the most-visited pages immediately accessible.

Finding

Weak Visual Hierarchy

Participants had trouble identifying what to do first on key pages. Post-test scores for ease of finding information were noticeably lower than expected satisfaction.

Redesign

Improved Layout and Emphasis

Adjusted spacing, sizing, and visual weight to ensure primary actions and key information were immediately scannable on first load.

Finding

Low Task Confidence

Pre-test questionnaires revealed low initial confidence, and post-test follow-up showed that unclear CTAs and sparse product information reinforced user hesitation throughout sessions.

Redesign

Clearer CTAs and Content

Rewrote and repositioned calls to action, and added supporting content to reduce uncertainty at key decision points and encourage users to move forward with confidence.
