Overview

As Campus Lead for Changemaker at ASU West Valley, I identified usability gaps on the program's website that created confusion for prospective students. This evaluation documented those issues and proposed actionable improvements.

Problem

  • Navigation structure made it hard to find key program information
  • Lack of transparency around application processes and expectations
  • Content hierarchy did not reflect user priorities

Goals

  • Identify and document usability breakdowns
  • Propose design improvements backed by evaluation methods
  • Improve clarity and trust for new visitors

Process

I applied structured UX evaluation methods to assess the site and build a case for change.

Methods

  • Heuristic evaluation using Nielsen's 10 principles
  • Content audit to assess information structure
  • Comparative review of similar program websites

Deliverables

  • Usability evaluation report
  • Presentation of findings and recommendations
  • Prioritized list of design and content improvements

Key Findings

Navigation Gaps

Users had difficulty locating application deadlines and program requirements due to inconsistent labeling and deep page nesting.

Transparency Issues

The application process lacked clear expectations, creating hesitation among prospective students. Outdated bullet points on several pages also misled users.

Content Hierarchy

The most important information was not visually prioritized, causing users to miss key details on first scan.

Recommendations

Simplified navigation labels, a clear application timeline, a prominent FAQ section, updated contact information, and deletion of misleading content.
